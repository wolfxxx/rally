The art of the getaway.

Your car keeps moving. Pick a direction before a junction and it will turn as soon as the road opens.

  • Arrows or WASDSteer through the streets. On touch screens, use the direction buttons.
  • Collect 10 yellow flagsClear the neighborhood and reach the next round. The special S flag doubles the value of flags collected afterward.
  • Space drops smokeStun pursuers behind you for 3 seconds. Each puff costs 4% fuel and needs a short cooldown.
  • Keep moving, keep fueledGreen fuel cans restore 30%. Avoid red cars and orange roadblocks. You have 3 lives.
  • P or Escape to pauseMusic and the chase pause when you leave the window. M toggles sound.
